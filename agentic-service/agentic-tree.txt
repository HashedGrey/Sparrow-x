agsvc-tree.txt is a commented source-tree walkthrough of SparrowX Agentic Service (AGSvc), intended
as structured context for an LLM to explain how the service works end to end. It maps packages, files,
and responsibilities across mission submission, validation, intent interpretation, planning, Embabel
agent execution, evidence collection, synthesis, citations, persistence, Temporal orchestration,
gRPC transport, configuration, and external service adapters. Comments describe why each component
exists and how it participates in the runtime flow.
